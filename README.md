## Setup

No environment variables and no keys. You need a browser, the pricing page, and a blank spreadsheet or text file.

## 1. Reading the plan table

Open [ahrefs.com/pricing](https://ahrefs.com/pricing). The crawled page starts with a cookie consent dialog and then a rotation of banners for Letaido and Firehose; dismiss the dialog and scroll to the plan cards. For each card, record: plan name, monthly price, annual price if shown, users included, and any usage or credit limit printed under the price. Then read the feature rows below the cards and mark which rows differ between the two plans you are choosing between. Ignore rows that are identical; they are not part of the decision.

## 2. Sizing a plan for one person

Read the [Starter plan post](https://ahrefs.com/blog/starter-plan/) on the Ahrefs blog. It is the vendor's own description of who the entry-level plan is for. Compare its description against your list from walkthrough 1: number of sites you actually check, how often, and whether anyone else needs a seat. If your real usage fits inside what the post describes, start there; the review date in walkthrough 1 is when you revisit.

## 3. Paying from outside the billing currency

The [Wise article on Ahrefs pricing](https://wise.com/gb/blog/ahrefs-pricing) ranks for this query because a lot of subscribers pay from the UK and elsewhere. The check: find the currency the pricing page shows you, find the currency your card is billed in, and ask your bank what it charges on foreign-currency card payments. Add that percentage to the plan price before comparing tiers. Whether a multi-currency account is worth it is your call; the point is to compare the price you will actually pay.

## 4. Free first: Firehose and Letaido

The pricing page promotes two products in its banners. Firehose is described as tracking "brand mentions and competitor updates in real time" with a "Get Firehose free" link; if mention tracking was a reason for a paid plan, try the [Firehose sign-up](https://signin.firehose.com/sign-up) first. Letaido is described as "the only AI marketing platform built on Ahrefs' proprietary web index" for "marketing reports, apps, and automations"; if your need is recurring reports rather than research seats, look at the [Letaido sign-up](https://signin.letaido.com/sign-up) before buying seats. Neither is priced on the Ahrefs pricing page, so their cost is a separate check.
